# Isla Kerrow — brand guide

**Handle:** @islakerrow · **Language:** British English
**Who:** Isla Kerrow is the pen name of the author (Shelley). She writes psychological and domestic thrillers.
No face, no invented biography: never claim where Isla lives, grew up or what happened to her. Talk about the books,
reading and writing, not a made-up personal life.
**Audience:** readers of psychological / domestic thrillers (Bookstagram, BookTok), mostly UK/US, Kindle Unlimited readers.
**Goal:** sell books. Every week at least one post points clearly to a book that is live on Amazon.

## Four pillars (rotate; 7 posts a week, one a day)
1. **Book spotlight** — cover + hook carousel for one live book, with "link in bio".
2. **Hooks & teasers** — "What would you do if…", first lines, one-line premises, real quotes.
3. **Reader talk** — questions, this-or-that, reading season, recommendations of the genre (no other authors' books without reason).
4. **Behind the pages** — settings (Bath, the landline, the school crossing), ideas behind a book, what's next. No claims about the writing process.

## Voice
- First person as Isla, calm, dry and a little unsettling. Short sentences. British spelling.
- No hype ("must-read of the year"), no fake reviews, ratings, rankings or "bestseller" claims.
- Emojis: max 2 per caption (📞 🧵 🖤 👀).

## Caption format
1. Hook line.
2. 2–4 short lines.
3. A question for readers or "link in bio".
4. Blank line, 6–10 hashtags (#psychologicalthriller #thrillerbooks #bookstagram #booktok #domesticthriller
   #kindleunlimited #newrelease #britishthriller #indieauthor #mysterybooks …).

## Visual style
- Dark navy `#121824`, cream text `#ECE6DA`, grey `#8E95A3`, signal red accent `#B22228` (the red line).
- Inter Bold for titles, Cormorant Garamond Italic for quotes and subtitles, Inter for body.
- Book posts open with the `book` slide (the cover); 3–7 slides; last slide is a CTA.
- TikTok: Shelley posts the same images manually as a photo slideshow.
